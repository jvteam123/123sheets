CULINARY PROCUREMENT & PROTOCOLS

Buttered Seafood

SECTOR 01 • 3.0 KG MIXED

  • • 1kg Shrimp, 1kg Crab, 1kg Mussels
  • • 2 Blocks Butter + 3 Heads Garlic
  • • 1L Sprite + Oyster Sauce

Method: Melt butter and sauté garlic/ginger. Steam crab/mussels in Sprite for 8m. Add shrimp and remaining butter. Reduce until liquid forms a thick glaze.

"Toss frequently to ensure even coating."

Sweet & Sour Chicken

SECTOR 02 • 2.0 KG CUTS

  • • 2kg Thigh/Breast cubes
  • • Bell Peppers, Carrots, Pineapple
  • • Banana Ketchup, Vinegar, Sugar

Method: Dredge chicken in cornstarch/flour and deep fry. Sauté veggies separately. Simmer ketchup, vinegar, and pineapple juice. Thicken with slurry, then toss in chicken.

Grilled Liempo

SECTOR 03 • 3.0 KG SLABS

  • • 3kg Pork Belly (1-inch thick)
  • • Calamansi, Soy Sauce, Garlic
  • • Brown Sugar, Sprite, Pepper

Method: Submerge pork in citrus-soy-sprite mix for 3.5 hours. Grill over medium-hot charcoal, basting every flip until caramelized.

Pro-Tip: Start Marinating at 2:30 PM SHARP.

Chicken Halang-Halang

SECTOR 04 • 2.0 KG SPICY

  • • 2kg Chicken serving pieces
  • • Coconut Milk (Thick & Thin)
  • • Lemongrass, Ginger, Labuyo

Method: Sauté heavy ginger and lemongrass. Sear chicken with patis. Simmer in thin milk until tender. Enrich with thick cream and chilies last.
